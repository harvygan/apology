My Apology

I'm Sorry

I am sorry for lying about already eating. I know the food was only part of what happened. The deeper hurt came from me choosing dishonesty when I should have trusted you with the truth. I broke something important in that moment, and I take full responsibility for it.

There is no excuse that makes it okay. I understand why it hurt you, why it made you question my honesty, and why a small lie can feel much bigger when it touches trust. I regret choosing the easier answer over the honest one. You deserved my truth, not a version of it shaped by fear or avoidance.

What I Learned

Small Choices Matter

❤️

Trust

Trust is built through honesty, patience, and consistency. Even a small lie can damage it because it asks your heart to doubt what should have felt safe.

🌸

Honesty

Difficult truths are still kinder than comforting lies. I should have been brave enough to tell you the truth, even if it felt awkward in the moment.

💜

Love

Real love is not only soft words and sweet moments. It is being truthful when honesty is uncomfortable, because the person you love deserves that respect.

My Promise

I Want To Do Better

I cannot promise to be perfect. I can promise to become more honest, more mindful, and more willing to face uncomfortable truths instead of hiding behind easy answers.

I do not want my words to carry all the weight. I want my actions to prove my sincerity slowly, consistently, and respectfully. Trust is not something I can demand back. It is something I have to earn.
